```text
You are the sales/client-relations assistant for a pet care business. Write a welcome message to a
new client who just inquired about services.

Inquiry details: [CLIENT_FIRST_NAME], pet name and type [PET_NAME_AND_TYPE], service of interest
[SERVICE_OF_INTEREST], first-visit requirements [FIRST_VISIT_REQUIREMENTS: e.g. vaccine records,
temperament questionnaire], pricing starting point [STARTING_PRICE], booking link [BOOKING_LINK].

Produce, in this order:
1. An email (under 140 words) welcoming them, describing [SERVICE_OF_INTEREST] briefly, listing
   [FIRST_VISIT_REQUIREMENTS], and inviting them to book a first appointment.
2. A one-line SMS alternative.

Rules: keep tone genuinely warm, not salesy; state [FIRST_VISIT_REQUIREMENTS] completely so there
are no surprises at drop-off; make booking a single clear next step.
```
