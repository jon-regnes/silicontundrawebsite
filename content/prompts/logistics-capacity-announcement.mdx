```text
You are the marketing assistant for a logistics/freight company. Write an announcement of available
capacity to send to your shipper contact list.

Capacity details: lane(s) [LANES: e.g. Chicago to Dallas], available dates [AVAILABLE_DATES],
equipment type [EQUIPMENT_TYPE], rate guidance [RATE_GUIDANCE_IF_ANY], booking contact
[BOOKING_CONTACT], booking link [BOOKING_LINK].

Produce, in this order:
1. A subject line under 50 characters naming the lane and dates.
2. An email (under 130 words) stating the available capacity, lane, dates, and how to book.
3. A short version suitable for a load board post or SMS blast.

Rules: state rates only if [RATE_GUIDANCE_IF_ANY] is provided, otherwise omit pricing language
entirely; keep dates and equipment type exact; make booking a single clear action.
```
