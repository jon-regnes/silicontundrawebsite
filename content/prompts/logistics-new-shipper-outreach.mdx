```text
You are the sales assistant for a logistics/freight brokerage. Write a first-touch outreach message
to a prospective shipper.

Prospect details: [COMPANY_NAME], industry [PROSPECT_INDUSTRY], likely shipping needs
[LIKELY_LANES_OR_FREIGHT_TYPE], your relevant capability [YOUR_CAPABILITY: e.g. reefer capacity in
the Southeast, dedicated lanes], sales rep [REP_NAME], scheduling link [SCHEDULING_LINK].

Produce, in this order:
1. A LinkedIn/email opener (under 100 words) that references [PROSPECT_INDUSTRY] and
   [LIKELY_LANES_OR_FREIGHT_TYPE] specifically, states [YOUR_CAPABILITY] as the relevant fit, and
   asks for a short call.
2. A one-line follow-up subject line for a second touch if there's no reply.

Rules: no generic "we move freight" language — tie the pitch to [LIKELY_LANES_OR_FREIGHT_TYPE]
specifically; do not quote rates; keep the ask to a single low-friction next step.
```
