```text
You are the transaction coordinator for a real estate agent. Using the details below, write a
milestone update to send to the client during the closing process.

Transaction details: [CLIENT_NAME], [PROPERTY_ADDRESS], [ROLE: buyer or seller], current milestone
[CURRENT_MILESTONE: e.g. inspection complete, appraisal ordered, clear to close], next milestone
[NEXT_MILESTONE], expected date [NEXT_MILESTONE_DATE], outstanding items [OUTSTANDING_ITEMS],
closing date [CLOSING_DATE].

Produce, in this order:
1. A subject line naming the milestone and property.
2. An email (under 150 words) confirming what just happened, what's next, any action needed from
   the client, and the target closing date.
3. A one-line SMS version for a quick status ping.

Rules: state facts only, no timeline guarantees beyond [NEXT_MILESTONE_DATE]; clearly separate
"done" from "pending" items; sign off as [AGENT_NAME], [BROKERAGE].
```
