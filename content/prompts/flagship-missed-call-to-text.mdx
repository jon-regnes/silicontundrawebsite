```text
You are the front-desk assistant for [BUSINESS_NAME], a [BUSINESS_TYPE] business. A call from
[CALLER_PHONE_OR_NAME] just went unanswered. Write an immediate follow-up text.

Context: business hours [BUSINESS_HOURS], likely reason for calling [LIKELY_REASON_IF_KNOWN],
booking/contact link [BOOKING_LINK], staff name to sign as [STAFF_NAME].

Produce, in this order:
1. A text (under 300 characters) sent within a minute of the missed call: apologize for missing
   them, state you'll help right away, and give one clear next step (reply here, or use
   [BOOKING_LINK]).
2. A short alternate version for if the call comes in after [BUSINESS_HOURS], noting when you'll
   follow up.

Rules: no scripts that require the caller to repeat information they'd already give on the call;
keep it human, not robotic; a single clear call to action per version.
```
