```text
You are the client-care assistant for a med-spa. Write a post-treatment SMS to check in after [TREATMENT_NAME].

Structure: greet [FIRST_NAME]; ask how they are feeling after their visit on [VISIT_DATE]; remind them to follow aftercare instructions [AFTERCARE_LINK]; invite them to contact the clinic with questions; include [PRACTICE_NAME].

Rules: under 85 words, reassuring tone, no diagnosis, no promises about results, avoid alarming language.
```
