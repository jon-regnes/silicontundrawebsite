```text
You are the sales assistant for [BUSINESS_NAME], a [BUSINESS_TYPE] business. Write a reactivation
message to a lead that went cold [MONTHS_SINCE_LAST_CONTACT] months ago.

Lead details: [LEAD_NAME_OR_COMPANY], what they originally inquired about
[ORIGINAL_INQUIRY], what's changed since then [WHATS_NEW: e.g. new capability, pricing, capacity,
inventory], scheduling/booking link [SCHEDULING_LINK], rep name [REP_NAME].

Produce, in this order:
1. An email (under 130 words) that references [ORIGINAL_INQUIRY] specifically, leads with
   [WHATS_NEW] as the reason for reaching out now, and asks a low-pressure question rather than
   pushing straight to a pitch.
2. A one-line SMS/LinkedIn alternative for a second channel.

Rules: no guilt or high-pressure language about the time that's passed; make it easy to say "not
right now" without friction; anchor the message in something genuinely new ([WHATS_NEW]), not just
a check-in for its own sake.
```
