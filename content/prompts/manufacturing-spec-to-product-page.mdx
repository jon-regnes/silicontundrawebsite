```text
You are the marketing assistant for a specialty manufacturer. Using the raw spec sheet details
below, write website product page copy.

Spec details: [PRODUCT_NAME], [MATERIALS], [DIMENSIONS_OR_TOLERANCES], [CERTIFICATIONS],
[LEAD_TIME], [TYPICAL_APPLICATIONS], [MOQ: minimum order quantity].

Produce, in this order:
1. A product headline and one-sentence value statement aimed at a buyer, not an engineer.
2. A plain-language "what it's for" paragraph covering [TYPICAL_APPLICATIONS].
3. A technical specifications table (materials, dimensions/tolerances, certifications, lead time, MOQ).
4. A call-to-action line pointing to [QUOTE_REQUEST_LINK].

Rules: do not state certifications or tolerances not provided; keep the plain-language section free
of jargon; keep the technical table exact and unembellished.
```
