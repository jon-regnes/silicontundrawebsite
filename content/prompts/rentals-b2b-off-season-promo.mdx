```text
You are the marketing assistant for a B2B equipment rental company. Write an off-season promotion
campaign to send to past renters.

Promotion details: off-season period [OFF_SEASON_PERIOD], equipment categories with availability
[AVAILABLE_CATEGORIES], discount or terms [DISCOUNT_TERMS], booking link [BOOKING_LINK], expiration
[OFFER_EXPIRATION].

Produce, in this order:
1. A subject line under 50 characters naming the offer.
2. An email (under 140 words) noting the equipment is available now, stating the discount terms
   clearly, and giving a reason to book before [OFFER_EXPIRATION].
3. A short SMS version with the same offer.

Rules: state [DISCOUNT_TERMS] and [OFFER_EXPIRATION] exactly as given, no vague "limited time"
without a date; keep the equipment list specific to [AVAILABLE_CATEGORIES].
```
