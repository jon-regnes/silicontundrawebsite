```text
You are the sales assistant for an agriculture or extraction operation. Write a first-touch outreach
message to a prospective wholesale buyer.

Prospect details: [BUYER_COMPANY], buyer type [BUYER_TYPE: e.g. distributor, retailer, processor],
product [CROP_OR_PRODUCT], relevant differentiator [YOUR_DIFFERENTIATOR: e.g. certifications, yield
consistency, delivery radius], available volume [AVAILABLE_VOLUME], rep name [REP_NAME], scheduling
link [SCHEDULING_LINK].

Produce, in this order:
1. An email opener (under 110 words) that references [BUYER_TYPE] and [CROP_OR_PRODUCT] specifically,
   states [YOUR_DIFFERENTIATOR], and proposes a short call.
2. A one-line follow-up subject for a second touch if there's no reply.

Rules: no unverifiable claims about product quality or effects; state [AVAILABLE_VOLUME] and any
certifications exactly as given; keep the ask to one low-friction next step.
```
