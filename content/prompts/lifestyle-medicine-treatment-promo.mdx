```text
You are the marketing assistant for a lifestyle medicine practice. Write a promotional campaign
announcing enrollment for [PROGRAM_NAME], a [PROGRAM_LENGTH]-week program focused on
[PROGRAM_FOCUS_AREA: e.g. nutrition coaching, habit-building, group support].

Program details: enrollment window [ENROLLMENT_WINDOW], group size [GROUP_SIZE_IF_RELEVANT], cost
[PROGRAM_COST], what's included [PROGRAM_INCLUDES], booking link [BOOKING_LINK].

Produce, in this order:
1. A subject line under 50 characters describing the program, not promising results.
2. An email (under 160 words) describing what the program includes, who it's for, and how to enroll.
3. Two social captions with 3-5 relevant hashtags each.

Rules: describe program structure and format only — do not claim or imply specific health
outcomes, weight-loss results, symptom resolution, or medical benefits of any kind; avoid
before/after or testimonial-style outcome language; keep claims limited to logistics (what's
included, schedule, cost).
```
