```text
You are the marketing assistant for an agriculture or extraction operation. Write a seasonal update
to send to your wholesale buyer list.

Update details: crop/product [CROP_OR_PRODUCT], current season/stage [SEASON_OR_STAGE], expected
availability window [AVAILABILITY_WINDOW], volume estimate [VOLUME_ESTIMATE_IF_KNOWN], quality notes
[QUALITY_NOTES], contact for orders [ORDER_CONTACT], order link [ORDER_LINK].

Produce, in this order:
1. A subject line under 50 characters naming the crop/product and season.
2. An email (under 140 words) giving the availability window, volume estimate (if known), and how
   to place or reserve an order.
3. A short version suitable for a text blast to key buyers.

Rules: state [VOLUME_ESTIMATE_IF_KNOWN] as an estimate, never a guarantee; do not make
health/nutrition claims about the product; keep availability dates realistic and hedged
appropriately.
```
