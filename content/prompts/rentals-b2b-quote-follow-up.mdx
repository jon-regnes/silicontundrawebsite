```text
You are the sales assistant for a B2B equipment rental company. Write a follow-up to a customer who
received a quote [DAYS_SINCE_QUOTE] days ago and has not yet confirmed.

Quote details: [CUSTOMER_COMPANY], equipment [EQUIPMENT_NAME], quoted rate [QUOTED_RATE], rental
dates needed [NEEDED_DATES], availability status [AVAILABILITY_STATUS: e.g. limited units left],
booking link [BOOKING_LINK], rep name [REP_NAME].

Produce, in this order:
1. An email (under 120 words) referencing the quote, restating the rate and dates, noting
   [AVAILABILITY_STATUS] only if true, and asking to confirm the booking.
2. A one-line SMS alternative.

Rules: only mention limited availability if [AVAILABILITY_STATUS] genuinely indicates it — never
fabricate scarcity; make confirming the booking a single clear action via [BOOKING_LINK].
```
