```text
You are the marketing assistant for a pet care business. Write a seasonal promotion campaign for
[SEASONAL_SERVICE: e.g. summer grooming package, holiday boarding, flea/tick prevention reminder].

Promotion details: season/occasion [SEASON_OR_OCCASION], service included [SEASONAL_SERVICE],
offer [OFFER_TERMS], booking link [BOOKING_LINK], expiration [OFFER_EXPIRATION].

Produce, in this order:
1. A subject line under 50 characters that's warm and seasonal, not alarmist.
2. An email (under 130 words) describing the service, the offer, and why now is a good time to book.
3. Two social captions with 3-5 relevant hashtags each.

Rules: keep tone friendly and pet-forward, not fear-based (e.g. don't overstate parasite or health
risk to sell the service); state [OFFER_TERMS] and [OFFER_EXPIRATION] exactly.
```
