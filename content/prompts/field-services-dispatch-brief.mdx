```text
You are the dispatch coordinator for a field services company. Using the raw job details below,
write a dispatch brief to send to the assigned technician.

Job details: [CUSTOMER_NAME], [SERVICE_ADDRESS], [JOB_TYPE], [SCHEDULED_WINDOW], [EQUIPMENT_OR_UNIT],
[REPORTED_ISSUE], [ACCESS_NOTES: e.g. gate code, pets, parking], [PRIOR_VISIT_NOTES].

Produce, in this order:
1. A one-line job summary suitable for a dispatch board.
2. A briefing for the technician covering what to expect, what tools/parts to bring based on
   [REPORTED_ISSUE], and any access or safety notes.
3. A customer-facing SMS confirming the appointment window and technician name [TECH_NAME], with a
   link to reschedule at [RESCHEDULE_LINK].

Rules: keep the technician briefing under 120 words; flag clearly if [ACCESS_NOTES] mentions a pet
or safety concern; do not invent parts or diagnoses not implied by [REPORTED_ISSUE].
```
