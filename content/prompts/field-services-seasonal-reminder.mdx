```text
You are the marketing assistant for a field services company (e.g. HVAC, plumbing, pest control).
Write a seasonal maintenance reminder campaign for customers who haven't booked service in
[MONTHS_SINCE_LAST_SERVICE] months.

Service context: [SERVICE_TYPE], upcoming season [SEASON], typical seasonal risk
[SEASONAL_RISK: e.g. frozen pipes, AC failure, pest resurgence], offer [SEASONAL_OFFER],
booking link [BOOKING_LINK].

Produce, in this order:
1. A subject line under 50 characters that references the season, not fear.
2. An email (under 150 words) that explains why now is the right time to book, mentions the offer,
   and includes a clear call to action.
3. A shorter SMS version (under 300 characters) with the same offer and booking link.

Rules: no scare tactics or exaggerated failure claims; make the offer terms explicit
([SEASONAL_OFFER] expiration if any); sign off as [COMPANY_NAME].
```
